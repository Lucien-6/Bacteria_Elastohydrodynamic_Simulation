Background研究背景

Why Simulate Bacteria Settling with Pili?为何模拟带纤毛细菌的沉降？

Bacteria swim, sink, and adhere in a world dominated by viscosity. At their scale the Reynolds number is essentially zero, so inertia vanishes and motion is governed by Stokes flow.

Their surface appendages — pili and flagella — are slender, flexible filaments that bend, twist, and (for flagella) are driven by rotary motors, strongly coupling to the fluid.

细菌在以黏性主导的世界中游动、沉降与黏附。在其尺度下雷诺数几乎为零，惯性可忽略，运动由 Stokes 流支配。

其表面附器——纤毛与鞭毛——是细长柔性丝，可弯曲、扭转，鞭毛还由旋转马达驱动，并与流体强烈耦合。

Modelling challenge建模挑战

  • Body and appendages share one fluid → fully coupled hydrodynamics.
  • Filaments are elastic & inextensible → Kirchhoff-rod mechanics.
  • Thermal fluctuations matter at micron scale → Brownian dynamics.
  • Geometry spans orders of magnitude (body µm vs. filament radius nm).
  • 胞体与附器共享同一流体 → 完全耦合的流体力学。
  • 丝弹性且不可伸长 → Kirchhoff 杆力学。
  • 微米尺度下热涨落重要 → 布朗动力学。
  • 几何尺度跨数量级（胞体 µm 与丝半径 nm）。

Coupling principle耦合原理

Hydrodynamics · 1a流体力学 · 1a

From Stokes Flow to the Regularized Stokeslet从 Stokes 流到正则化 Stokeslet

At zero Reynolds number the flow obeys the steady Stokes equations — linear, instantaneous, inertia-free:

零雷诺数下流动服从稳态 Stokes 方程——线性、瞬时、无惯性：

\[ \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{u} - \nabla p + \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}, \qquad \nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{u}=0 \]

A singular point force is replaced by a smooth, unit-weight blob \(\phi_{\varepsilon}\) (Cortez–Fauci–Medovikov 2005):

奇异点力由一个光滑、单位权重的 blob \(\phi_{\varepsilon}\) 取代（Cortez–Fauci–Medovikov 2005）：

\[ \phi_{\varepsilon}(r) = \frac{15\,\varepsilon^{4}}{8\pi\,(r^{2}+\varepsilon^{2})^{7/2}}, \quad \int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\!\phi_{\varepsilon}\,dV = 1 \]

The velocity from one regularized force \(\mathbf{f}_0\) at \(\mathbf{y}_0\) (with \(\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0,\ r=\lVert\mathbf{r}\rVert\)):

位于 \(\mathbf{y}_0\) 的单个正则化力 \(\mathbf{f}_0\) 产生的速度（\(\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0,\ r=\lVert\mathbf{r}\rVert\)）：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\,\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}(\mathbf{r})\,\mathbf{f}_0 \]
\[ S^{\varepsilon}_{ij} = \frac{(r^{2}+2\varepsilon^{2})\,\delta_{ij} + r_i r_j}{(r^{2}+\varepsilon^{2})^{3/2}} \]

As \(\varepsilon\to0\) the classical Stokeslet is recovered; for \(\varepsilon>0\) the field is smooth and bounded at \(r=0\), removing the singularity that plagues boundary-integral methods.

当 \(\varepsilon\to0\) 恢复经典 Stokeslet；当 \(\varepsilon>0\) 时速度场在 \(r=0\) 处光滑有界，消除了边界积分法的奇异性。

RSM Discretization on the Body Surface胞体表面的 RSM 离散

The body surface carries \(N\) regularized forces \(\mathbf{f}_n\). By linearity of Stokes flow, velocities superpose into a dense mobility relation:

胞体表面布置 \(N\) 个正则化力 \(\mathbf{f}_n\)。由 Stokes 流的线性性，速度叠加为稠密的迁移率关系：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}_m) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}(\mathbf{x}_m-\mathbf{x}_n)\,\mathbf{f}_n \;\equiv\; (\mathbf{M}\,\mathbf{f})_m \]

Because the body is rigid, every surface point velocity is tied to six body DOF (\(\mathbf{U},\boldsymbol{\Omega}\)) — this is the \(\mathbf{Q}\) block of the coupled solve:

由于胞体刚性，每个表面点速度都由 6 个胞体自由度（\(\mathbf{U},\boldsymbol{\Omega}\)）决定——即耦合求解中的 \(\mathbf{Q}\) 块：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}_n) = \mathbf{U} + \boldsymbol{\Omega}\times(\mathbf{x}_n-\mathbf{X}_{\text{cm}}) \]

Body regularization (numerical)胞体正则化（数值）

\[ \varepsilon_{\text{body}} = h_{\text{mean}}/2.6667 \]

\(h_{\text{mean}}\) = mean nearest-neighbour spacing of body points; a resolution parameter calibrated by a Stokes-sphere sweep (overridable).

\(h_{\text{mean}}\) = 胞体点平均最近邻间距；是由 Stokes 球扫描标定的分辨率参数（可覆盖）。

Root–body gap根部–胞体间隙

\[ \mathrm{dis}=1.1\,(\varepsilon_{\text{body}}+\varepsilon_{\text{pili}})\,L_0 \]

Separates regularized kernels at each pilus root, avoiding near-singular body–pilus interactions.

在各纤毛根部分离正则化核，避免胞体–纤毛近奇异相互作用。

Pili — Regularized Stokeslet Segments (RSS)纤毛 — 正则化 Stokeslet 段 (RSS)

Rather than lumping force at points, Cortez (2018) distributes it continuously along each straight segment, parameterized by \(t\in[0,1]\), length \(L\):

Cortez (2018) 不把力集中到点上，而是沿每条直段连续分布，以 \(t\in[0,1]\) 参数化，段长 \(L\)：

\[ \mathbf{y}(t) = \mathbf{y}_0 + t\,(\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0) \]

with a linear force density between the two endpoints:

端点之间取线性力密度：

\[ \mathbf{g}(t) = (1-t)\,\mathbf{f}_0 + t\,\mathbf{f}_1 \]

The induced velocity is the exact line integral of the regularized kernel along the segment:

诱导速度是正则化核沿该段的精确线积分：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\!\int_0^1\! \mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}\!\big(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\big)\,\mathbf{g}(t)\,L\,dt = \mathbf{M}_1\mathbf{f}_0 + \mathbf{M}_2\mathbf{f}_1 \]

Integrating along the segment (not point quadrature) decouples segment length from \(\varepsilon\): ~10 segments/µm stay accurate. Here \(\varepsilon_{\text{pili}}=r_{\text{pili}}/L_0\) is the physical filament radius — not a numerical knob.

沿段积分（而非点求积）使段长与 \(\varepsilon\) 解耦：约 10 段/µm 仍精确。此处 \(\varepsilon_{\text{pili}}=r_{\text{pili}}/L_0\) 是物理丝半径——并非数值参数。

RSS Segment Integrals & Twist CouplingRSS 段积分与扭转耦合

Since \(\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\rVert^2\) is quadratic in \(t\), define

由于 \(\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\rVert^2\) 是 \(t\) 的二次式，定义

\[ R(t)^2 = \lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\rVert^2 + \varepsilon^2 \]

All segment matrices reduce to one small family of 1-D integrals:

所有段矩阵都归结为一组一维积分：

\[ T_{q,n}(\mathbf{x}) = \int_0^1 \frac{t^{\,q}}{R(t)^{\,n}}\,dt,\quad n\in\{1,3,5\},\ q\in\{0,1,2\} \]

\(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) combine the \(\delta_{ij}\) and dyadic \((\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})^{\mathsf T}\) parts weighted by \(T_{q,n}\); these admit closed forms and an \(n\)-recurrence.

\(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) 由 \(\delta_{ij}\) 项与并矢 \((\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y})^{\mathsf T}\) 项按 \(T_{q,n}\) 加权组合；后者有闭式解及关于 \(n\) 的递推。

Regularized rotlet segments正则化 rotlet 段

A twisting filament adds a rotlet segment built from the same \(T_{q,n}\) machinery:

扭转的丝再叠加一个由相同 \(T_{q,n}\) 构件搭建的 rotlet 段：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = -L\big(c_a[\mathbf{x}_0]_{\times} + c_b[\mathbf{v}]_{\times}\big)\boldsymbol{\tau} \]

\(\boldsymbol{\tau}\) = segment torque density, \([\cdot]_\times\) = skew operator, \(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\); coefficients \(c_a,c_b\) are combinations of \(T_{q,n}\).

\(\boldsymbol{\tau}\) = 段力矩密度，\([\cdot]_\times\) = 反对称算子，\(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\)；系数 \(c_a,c_b\) 为 \(T_{q,n}\) 的组合。

For bacterial pili this twist–translation coupling scales with high powers of \(\varepsilon\) — numerically tiny, but it keeps the formulation theoretically complete.

对细菌纤毛，该扭转–平动耦合按 \(\varepsilon\) 的高次幂衰减——数值上极小，但保证理论表述完整。

One Linear Solve per RHS Evaluation每次右端求值解一次线性系统

\[ \mathbf{L}\,\dot{\mathbf{z}} = \mathbf{R}, \qquad \mathbf{L} = -\,\mathbf{B}\,\mathbf{A}\,\mathbf{Q} \]
\[ \mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q} = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{M}_{\text{weighted}}^{-1}\,\mathbf{Q}_{\text{trans,corr}} \\[4pt] \widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{twist}}\,\mathbf{Q}_{\text{twist}} \end{bmatrix} \]
\[ \mathbf{Q}_{\text{trans,corr}} = \mathbf{Q}_{\text{trans}} - \mathbf{M}_{\text{rotlet}}\,\widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{twist}}\,\mathbf{Q}_{\text{twist}} \]

Solved by backslash / LU factorization — never explicit inversion.

用反斜杠 / LU 分解求解 —— 从不显式求逆。

Block meaning各块含义

  • Q_global — generalized rates → node velocities.
  • M_weighted — full mobility (body RSM + pili RSS).
  • A_twist — torsional resistance operator.
  • B_global — forces/torques → global force, moment & pilus moment-balance rows.
  • R — gravity, elastic/active/motor moments, flow correction.
  • Q_global — 广义速率 → 节点速度。
  • M_weighted — 完整迁移率（胞体 RSM + 纤毛 RSS）。
  • A_twist — 扭转阻力算子。
  • B_global — 力/力矩 → 整体力、力矩与纤毛力矩平衡行。
  • R — 重力、弹性/主动/马达力矩、流场修正。

Darboux (rotation) vector and director evolution:

Darboux（旋转）向量与 director 演化：

\[ \mathbf{U} = \kappa_1\mathbf{d}_1 + \kappa_2\mathbf{d}_2 + \kappa_3\mathbf{d}_3 \]
\[ \mathbf{d}_{\alpha}' = \mathbf{U}\times\mathbf{d}_{\alpha} \]

Primes denote arclength derivatives.

撇号表示对弧长求导。

Curvature components (relative to intrinsic \(\kappa_{\cdot,0}\)):

曲率分量（相对内禀值 \(\kappa_{\cdot,0}\)）：

\[ \kappa_1 = \mathbf{d}_3\!\cdot\!\mathbf{d}_2' - \kappa_{1,0} \] \[ \kappa_2 = \mathbf{d}_1\!\cdot\!\mathbf{d}_3' - \kappa_{2,0} \] \[ \kappa_3 = \frac{\mathbf{d}_2\!\cdot\!\mathbf{d}_1' - \kappa_{3,0}}{1+\sigma} \]

Twist factor \(1/(1+\sigma)\) is exactly \(GJ/EI\) for a circular cross-section (\(J=2I,\ G=E/[2(1+\sigma)]\)). Intrinsic curvature: frozen·zero·constant·helix. Active moments: off · traveling wave · custom.

扭转因子 \(1/(1+\sigma)\) 对圆截面恰为 \(GJ/EI\)（\(J=2I,\ G=E/[2(1+\sigma)]\)）。内禀曲率：frozen·zero·constant·helix。主动力矩：关闭 · 行波 · 自定义。

clamped

free_torsion

Conservation-consistent motor守恒一致的马达

In free_torsion, the root \(\mathbf{d}_3\) moment row may receive elastic torsion, an active \(\mathbf{d}_3\) moment, and a constant motor torque. No explicit motor reaction term is injected into \(\mathbf{R}_{4:6}\): those rows are the angular-momentum balance of the whole coupled system, so an internal body↔pilus torque cancels in the sum. The body's angular response emerges implicitly from the coupled Stokes solve.

在 free_torsion 下，根部 \(\mathbf{d}_3\) 力矩行可接收弹性扭转、主动 \(\mathbf{d}_3\) 力矩与常值马达力矩。不向 \(\mathbf{R}_{4:6}\) 显式注入马达反作用项：这些行是整个耦合系统的角动量平衡，胞体↔纤毛间的内部力矩在求和中相消。胞体的角响应由耦合 Stokes 求解隐式给出。

Background Flow背景流

A prescribed steady flow is incorporated through a hydrodynamic correction to the RHS:

给定的稳态流场通过对右端项的流体力学修正引入：

\[ \mathbf{R} \;\leftarrow\; \mathbf{R} - \mathbf{B}\,\mathbf{A}^{-1}\,\mathbf{u}_{\text{bg}} \]

off

Quiescent fluid; no background flow.

静止流体；无背景流。

uniform

Constant velocity, user-defined direction.

恒定速度，用户定义方向。

shear_z

Finite-thickness clipped shear layer.

有限厚度截断剪切层。

Background velocities use the same scales: \(\mathbf{u}_{\text{bg}}^{\ast}=\mathbf{u}_{\text{bg}}/U_0\).

背景速度采用相同尺度：\(\mathbf{u}_{\text{bg}}^{\ast}=\mathbf{u}_{\text{bg}}/U_0\)。

Brownian Dynamics (Operator-Split)布朗动力学（算符分裂）

  1. Integrate the deterministic coupled system with ode15s.
  2. At each Brownian node, compute the full-system effective body mobility.
  3. Apply a correlated 6-D translation–rotation increment.
  1. 用 ode15s 积分确定性耦合系统。
  2. 在每个布朗节点计算全系统有效胞体迁移率。
  3. 施加关联的 6 维平动–转动增量。

Probe \(\mathbf{L}\) with six unit generalized body loads:

用 6 个单位广义胞体载荷探测 \(\mathbf{L}\)：

\[ \mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}(:,i) = \big(\mathbf{L}^{-1}(-\mathbf{e}_i)\big)_{1:6},\ i=1,\dots,6 \]

After dimensional conversion (fluctuation–dissipation):

经量纲转换后（涨落–耗散）：

\[ \mathbf{D}_{\text{eff}} = k_B T\,\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff,dim}} \]
\[ \mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}^{\mathsf T} = 2\,\mathbf{D}_{\text{eff}} \]
\[ \delta\mathbf{U} = \mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\,\boldsymbol{\eta}\,\sqrt{\Delta t_B},\ \ \boldsymbol{\eta}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I}_6) \]

Optional fixman_midpoint re-evaluates mobility at a stochastic midpoint to approximate the Itô drift (2× cost).

可选 fixman_midpoint 在随机中点重算迁移率以近似 Itô 漂移（2 倍开销）。

Time-Integration Pipeline时间积分流水线

Per RHS — ode_coupled_rhs.m每次右端 — ode_coupled_rhs.m

  1. reconstruct_geometry: \(\mathbf{Z}\to\) lab-frame points & directors.
  2. build_coupled_lin_sys: assemble \(\mathbf{L}=-\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\) (cached LU).
  3. Assemble \(\mathbf{R}\): gravity, elastic + active + motor moments, flow correction.
  4. Apply root BCs (clamped / free-torsion).
  5. Solve \(\mathbf{L}\dot{\mathbf z}=\mathbf{R}\); pack \(\dot{\mathbf Z}\).
  1. reconstruct_geometry：\(\mathbf{Z}\to\) 实验室系坐标与 director。
  2. build_coupled_lin_sys：组装 \(\mathbf{L}=-\mathbf{B}\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\)（缓存 LU）。
  3. 组装 \(\mathbf{R}\)：重力、弹性 + 主动 + 马达力矩、流场修正。
  4. 施加根部边界条件（clamped / free-torsion）。
  5. 求解 \(\mathbf{L}\dot{\mathbf z}=\mathbf{R}\)；打包 \(\dot{\mathbf Z}\)。

Outer loop — run_simulation.m外层循环 — run_simulation.m

  • Adaptive ode15s with event-driven segmenting: stop on Euler-angle singularity or time limit.
  • adaptive_basis_check: rotate per-pilus basis away from \(\theta\to0,\pi\).
  • Re-normalize the body quaternion each segment.
  • Inject correlated Brownian kicks at Brownian nodes.
  • Post-process → units, derived quantities, exports.
  • 自适应 ode15s + 事件驱动分段：遇 Euler 角奇点或超时即停。
  • adaptive_basis_check：将逐纤毛基底旋转远离 \(\theta\to0,\pi\)。
  • 每段后重新归一化胞体四元数。
  • 在布朗节点注入关联布朗扰动。
  • 后处理 → 单位、导出量、输出文件。
