Background研究背景

Why Simulate Bacteria Settling with Pili?为何模拟带纤毛细菌的沉降？

Bacteria swim, sink, and adhere in a world dominated by viscosity. At their scale the Reynolds number is essentially zero, so inertia vanishes and motion is governed by Stokes flow.

Their surface appendages — pili and flagella — are slender, flexible filaments that bend, twist, and (for flagella) are driven by rotary motors, strongly coupling to the fluid.

细菌在以黏性主导的世界中游动、沉降与黏附。在其尺度下雷诺数几乎为零，惯性可忽略，运动由 Stokes 流支配。

其表面附器——纤毛与鞭毛——是细长柔性丝，可弯曲、扭转，鞭毛还由旋转马达驱动，并与流体强烈耦合。

Modelling challenge建模挑战

  • Body and appendages share one fluid → fully coupled hydrodynamics.
  • Filaments are elastic & inextensible → Kirchhoff-rod mechanics.
  • Thermal fluctuations matter at micron scale → Brownian dynamics.
  • Geometry spans orders of magnitude (body µm vs. filament radius nm).
  • 胞体与附器共享同一流体 → 完全耦合的流体力学。
  • 丝弹性且不可伸长 → Kirchhoff 杆力学。
  • 微米尺度下热涨落重要 → 布朗动力学。
  • 几何尺度跨数量级（胞体 µm 与丝半径 nm）。

Global State Vector and Body Orientation全局状态向量与胞体姿态

The full state stacks body position, the body orientation quaternion, and each pilus rod's Euler-angle fields:

完整状态堆叠了胞体位置、胞体姿态四元数，以及每根纤毛杆的 Euler 角场：

\[ \mathbf{Z} = \big[\, \mathbf{X}_{\text{cm}},\ \mathbf{q},\ \boldsymbol{\theta}_1,\boldsymbol{\phi}_1,\boldsymbol{\psi}_1,\ \ldots,\ \boldsymbol{\theta}_M,\boldsymbol{\phi}_M,\boldsymbol{\psi}_M \,\big] \]

The quaternion \(\mathbf{q}\) (body→lab rotation) is advanced by the Hamilton product:

四元数 \(\mathbf{q}\)（胞体→实验室系旋转）由 Hamilton 乘积推进：

\[ \frac{d\mathbf{q}}{dt} = \tfrac12 \begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ \boldsymbol{\Omega} \end{bmatrix} \otimes \mathbf{q} \]

\(\boldsymbol{\Omega}\) = body angular velocity in the lab frame, from z_dot(4:6).

\(\boldsymbol{\Omega}\) = 实验室系下的胞体角速度，取自 z_dot(4:6)。

Avoiding Gimbal Lock — Per-Pilus Adaptive Basis规避万向死锁 — 逐纤毛自适应基

Each pilus frame is parametrized by Euler angles \((\theta,\phi,\psi)\) relative to a per-pilus reference basis \(\mathbf{R}_k\); \(\theta\) is the tilt of the tangent \(\mathbf{d}_3\) from that basis's pole.

每根纤毛的标架由相对其参考基 \(\mathbf{R}_k\) 的 Euler 角 \((\theta,\phi,\psi)\) 参数化；\(\theta\) 为切向 \(\mathbf{d}_3\) 偏离该基极轴的倾角。

The singularity

奇异性

As the tangent nears a pole (\(\theta\to 0\) or \(\pi\)), \(\phi\) and \(\psi\) become indistinguishable — classic gimbal lock — and the angular-velocity → Euler-rate map carries a diverging \(1/\sin\theta\) factor, making the ODE stiff:

当切向逼近极轴（\(\theta\to 0\) 或 \(\pi\)）时，\(\phi\) 与 \(\psi\) 不可区分 —— 即万向死锁 —— 角速度 → Euler 速率映射含发散的 \(1/\sin\theta\) 因子，使 ODE 变刚性：

\[ \dot{\boldsymbol{\Theta}} = \mathbf{J}_{\text{euler}}(\theta)\,\boldsymbol{\omega},\quad \lVert\mathbf{J}_{\text{euler}}\rVert \sim \tfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\to\infty \]

The fix (Walker 2020)

解决（Walker 2020）

Each pilus carries its own basis \(\mathbf{R}_k\) (params.rot{k}, initially \(\mathbf{I}\)). An ode15s event halts integration just before any node reaches a pole:

每根纤毛各自携带参考基 \(\mathbf{R}_k\)（params.rot{k}，初始为 \(\mathbf{I}\)）。ode15s 事件在任一节点到达极点前中止积分：

\[ \min_{k,i}\big(\theta_{k,i},\,\pi-\theta_{k,i}\big) < \tfrac{\delta}{2},\ \ \delta=\pi/20 \]

adaptive_basis_check.m then re-poles only the offending pilus — pick the axis farthest from all current tangents, reflect into it (Householder), recompute \((\theta,\phi,\psi)\), re-orthonormalize \(\mathbf{R}_k\) (SVD), and restart:

adaptive_basis_check.m 随即仅对该纤毛重选极轴 —— 取离所有当前切向最远的方向，反射到该基（Householder），重算 \((\theta,\phi,\psi)\)，对 \(\mathbf{R}_k\) 重正交化（SVD）后重启：

\[ \hat{\mathbf z}_{\text{new}}=\arg\max_{\hat{\mathbf v}}\,\min_i\lVert\hat{\mathbf v}\mp\mathbf{d}_3^{(i)}\rVert,\qquad \mathbf{R}_{\text{local}}=\mathbf{I}+2\mathbf{K}^2,\qquad \mathbf{R}_k\leftarrow\mathbf{R}_{\text{local}}\mathbf{R}_k \]

Per-pilus & on-demand: only the pilus nearing a pole is re-based, so the overhead is negligible. Curvature is built from director dot products, hence basis-invariant — the physics and intrinsic curvature \(\kappa_{i,0}\) are unchanged. This is exactly the event-driven segmenting of the time-integration loop.

逐纤毛、按需触发：仅对逼近极点的纤毛换基，开销可忽略。曲率由 director 点积构成，故与基无关 —— 物理与内禀曲率 \(\kappa_{i,0}\) 保持不变。这正是时间积分循环中的事件驱动分段。

From Stokes Flow to the Regularized Stokeslet从 Stokes 流到正则化 Stokeslet

At zero Reynolds number the flow obeys the steady Stokes equations — linear, instantaneous, inertia-free:

零雷诺数下流动服从稳态 Stokes 方程——线性、瞬时、无惯性：

\[ \mu\nabla^2\mathbf{u} - \nabla p + \mathbf{f} = \mathbf{0}, \qquad \nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{u}=0 \]

A singular point force is replaced by a smooth, unit-weight blob \(\phi_{\varepsilon}\) (Cortez–Fauci–Medovikov 2005):

奇异点力由一个光滑、单位权重的 blob \(\phi_{\varepsilon}\) 取代（Cortez–Fauci–Medovikov 2005）：

\[ \phi_{\varepsilon}(r) = \frac{15\,\varepsilon^{4}}{8\pi\,(r^{2}+\varepsilon^{2})^{7/2}}, \quad \int_{\mathbb{R}^3}\!\phi_{\varepsilon}\,dV = 1 \]

The velocity from one regularized force \(\mathbf{f}_0\) at \(\mathbf{y}_0\) (with \(\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0,\ r=\lVert\mathbf{r}\rVert\)):

位于 \(\mathbf{y}_0\) 的单个正则化力 \(\mathbf{f}_0\) 产生的速度（\(\mathbf{r}=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0,\ r=\lVert\mathbf{r}\rVert\)）：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\,\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}(\mathbf{r})\,\mathbf{f}_0 \]
\[ S^{\varepsilon}_{ij} = \frac{(r^{2}+2\varepsilon^{2})\,\delta_{ij} + r_i r_j}{(r^{2}+\varepsilon^{2})^{3/2}} \]

As \(\varepsilon\to0\) the classical Stokeslet is recovered; for \(\varepsilon>0\) the field is smooth and bounded at \(r=0\), removing the singularity that plagues boundary-integral methods.

当 \(\varepsilon\to0\) 恢复经典 Stokeslet；当 \(\varepsilon>0\) 时速度场在 \(r=0\) 处光滑有界，消除了边界积分法的奇异性。

RSM Discretization on the Body Surface胞体表面的 RSM 离散

The body surface carries \(N\) regularized forces \(\mathbf{f}_n\). By linearity of Stokes flow, velocities superpose into a dense mobility relation:

胞体表面布置 \(N\) 个正则化力 \(\mathbf{f}_n\)。由 Stokes 流的线性性，速度叠加为稠密的迁移率关系：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}_m) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}(\mathbf{x}_m-\mathbf{x}_n)\,\mathbf{f}_n \;\equiv\; (\mathbf{M}\,\mathbf{f})_m \]

Because the body is rigid, every surface point velocity is tied to six body DOF (\(\mathbf{U},\boldsymbol{\Omega}\)) — this is the \(\mathbf{Q}\) block of the coupled solve:

由于胞体刚性，每个表面点速度都由 6 个胞体自由度（\(\mathbf{U},\boldsymbol{\Omega}\)）决定——即耦合求解中的 \(\mathbf{Q}\) 块：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}_n) = \mathbf{U} + \boldsymbol{\Omega}\times(\mathbf{x}_n-\mathbf{X}_{\text{cm}}) \]

Body regularization (numerical)胞体正则化（数值）

\[ \varepsilon_{\text{body}} = h_{\text{mean}}/2.6667 \]

\(h_{\text{mean}}\) = mean nearest-neighbour spacing of body points; a resolution parameter calibrated by a Stokes-sphere sweep (overridable).

\(h_{\text{mean}}\) = 胞体点平均最近邻间距；是由 Stokes 球扫描标定的分辨率参数（可覆盖）。

Root–body gap根部–胞体间隙

\[ \mathrm{dis}=1.1\,(\varepsilon_{\text{body}}+\varepsilon_{\text{pili}})\,L_0 \]

Separates regularized kernels at each pilus root, avoiding near-singular body–pilus interactions.

在各纤毛根部分离正则化核，避免胞体–纤毛近奇异相互作用。

Pili — Regularized Stokeslet Segments (RSS)纤毛 — 正则化 Stokeslet 段 (RSS)

RSM lumps force at points — fine for the body, but a slender pilus is better described by force distributed continuously along each straight segment \(\mathbf{y}_0\!\to\!\mathbf{y}_1\) (Cortez 2018). With \(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\), \(t\in[0,1]\), length \(L\):

RSM 把力集中到点上——对胞体适用，但细长纤毛更宜将力沿每条直段 \(\mathbf{y}_0\!\to\!\mathbf{y}_1\) 连续分布（Cortez 2018）。记 \(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\)、\(t\in[0,1]\)、段长 \(L\)：

\[ \mathbf{y}(t) = \mathbf{y}_0 + t\,\mathbf{v},\qquad \mathbf{f}(t) = (1-t)\,\mathbf{f}_0 + t\,\mathbf{f}_1 \]

i.e. a linear force density fixed by the two endpoint forces \(\mathbf{f}_0,\mathbf{f}_1\).

即由两端点力 \(\mathbf{f}_0,\mathbf{f}_1\) 确定的线性力密度。

The induced velocity is the exact line integral of the same regularized kernel \(\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}\), which evaluates in closed form to a two-block relation:

诱导速度是同一正则化核 \(\mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}\) 沿该段的精确线积分，可闭式求得为两块关系：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{1}{8\pi\mu}\!\int_0^1\! \mathbf{S}^{\varepsilon}\!\big(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\big)\,\mathbf{f}(t)\,L\,dt = \mathbf{M}_1\mathbf{f}_0 + \mathbf{M}_2\mathbf{f}_1 \]

Integrating along the segment (not point quadrature) decouples the segment length \(h\) from \(\varepsilon\). Here \(\varepsilon_{\text{pili}}=r_{\text{pili}}/L_0\) is the physical filament radius (effective tube radius \(r_e\approx0.97\,\varepsilon\)), so ~10 segments/µm stay accurate where point-RSM would need hundreds of nodes.

沿段积分（而非点求积）使段长 \(h\) 与 \(\varepsilon\) 解耦。此处 \(\varepsilon_{\text{pili}}=r_{\text{pili}}/L_0\) 是物理丝半径（等效管半径 \(r_e\approx0.97\,\varepsilon\)），故约 10 段/µm 即可精确，而点式 RSM 需数百节点。

RSS — How \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) Are ComputedRSS — \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) 如何计算

With \(R(t)^2=\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\rVert^2+\varepsilon^2\), every term of the integrand is a polynomial in \(t\) times a power of \(R\). The integral therefore reduces to one scalar family:

记 \(R(t)^2=\lVert\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}(t)\rVert^2+\varepsilon^2\)，被积式每项都是 \(t\) 的多项式乘 \(R\) 的幂，积分归约为同一组标量积分：

\[ T_{n,q} = \int_0^1 t^{\,n} R(t)^{\,q}\,dt,\quad q\in\{-1,-3,-5\} \]

All \(T_{n,q}\) follow one recursion in \(n\), seeded by the closed forms \(T_{0,-1},T_{0,-3}\) — no quadrature, no special functions:

所有 \(T_{n,q}\) 由关于 \(n\) 的一条递推给出，并以闭式 \(T_{0,-1},T_{0,-3}\) 起步 —— 无需求积、无特殊函数：

\[ T_{n,q} = \frac{t^{\,n-1}R^{q+2}}{(q{+}2)L^2}\Big|_0^1 - \frac{n{-}1}{(q{+}2)L^2}\,T_{n-2,q+2} - \frac{(\mathbf{x}_0\!\cdot\!\mathbf{v})}{L^2}\,T_{n-1,q} \]

Collecting terms, the two \(3{\times}3\) blocks combine the \(T_{n,q}\) with the dyads \(\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T},\ \mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}{+}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T},\ \mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}\) (\(\mathbf{x}_0=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0\)) — these are the \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) from the previous slide:

整理后，两个 \(3{\times}3\) 块由 \(T_{n,q}\) 与并矢 \(\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T},\ \mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}{+}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T},\ \mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}\) 组合（\(\mathbf{x}_0=\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{y}_0\)）—— 即上一页的 \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\)：

\[ \mathbf{M}_2 = L\big[(T_{1,-1}{+}\varepsilon^2T_{1,-3})\mathbf{I} + T_{1,-3}\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T} + T_{2,-3}(\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}{+}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T}) + T_{3,-3}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}\big] \]
\[ \mathbf{M}_1 = L\big[(T_{0,-1}{+}\varepsilon^2T_{0,-3})\mathbf{I} + T_{0,-3}\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T} + T_{1,-3}(\mathbf{x}_0\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}{+}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{x}_0^{\mathsf T}) + T_{2,-3}\mathbf{v}\mathbf{v}^{\mathsf T}\big] - \mathbf{M}_2 \]

Regularized Rotlet Segments & Twist Coupling正则化 rotlet 段与扭转耦合

A twisting filament also drives flow. The velocity from a linear torque density \(\boldsymbol{\tau}=\boldsymbol{\tau}_a+t\,\boldsymbol{\tau}_b\) along the segment is the curl of the Stokeslet field:

扭转的丝同样驱动流动。沿段的线性力矩密度 \(\boldsymbol{\tau}=\boldsymbol{\tau}_a+t\,\boldsymbol{\tau}_b\) 产生的速度是 Stokeslet 场的旋度：

\[ 8\pi\mu\,\mathbf{u} = L\!\int_0^1\!\Big(\tfrac{2}{R^3}+\tfrac{3\varepsilon^2}{R^5}\Big)(\boldsymbol{\tau}\times\mathbf{x})\,dt \]

which collapses onto the same \(T_{n,q}\) family (now with \(q=-3,-5\)):

它同样归约到相同的 \(T_{n,q}\) 族（此时 \(q=-3,-5\)）：

In the assembled system this becomes the project's compact skew-operator form:

在组装系统中即为本项目的紧凑反对称算子形式：

\[ \mathbf{u}(\mathbf{x}) = -L\big(c_a[\mathbf{x}_0]_{\times} + c_b[\mathbf{v}]_{\times}\big)\boldsymbol{\tau} \]

\([\cdot]_\times\) = skew operator, \(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\); coefficients \(c_a,c_b\) are linear combinations of \(T_{n,-3},T_{n,-5}\). This is the \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{rotlet}}\) block.

\([\cdot]_\times\) = 反对称算子，\(\mathbf{v}=\mathbf{y}_1-\mathbf{y}_0\)；系数 \(c_a,c_b\) 为 \(T_{n,-3},T_{n,-5}\) 的线性组合，即 \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{rotlet}}\) 块。

For bacterial pili this twist–translation coupling scales with high powers of \(\varepsilon\) — numerically tiny, but it keeps the formulation theoretically complete and the rotlet provides the torsional resistance feeding \(\widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{twist}}\).

For \(M\) pili with \(N_k\) segments and \(N_{\text{tot}}\) hydrodynamic points, three sizes drive every block (build_coupled_lin_sys.m):

\(\mathbf{Q}\) — rates → velocities— 速率 → 速度

\([n_{\text{vel}}\times n_{\text{state}}]\). Body block is the rigid Jacobian \([\mathbf{I}\,\;-[\mathbf{r}_i]_\times]\); each pilus block maps Euler-rates through directors, scaled by \(\ell_k/N_k\), rotated to the lab frame.

\([n_{\text{vel}}\times n_{\text{state}}]\)。胞体块为刚体 Jacobian \([\mathbf{I}\,\;-[\mathbf{r}_i]_\times]\)；各纤毛块经 director 将 Euler 速率映射为速度，乘 \(\ell_k/N_k\) 并旋转到实验室系。

\(\mathbf{M}\) — mobility— 迁移率

\([3N_{\text{tot}}\times 3N_{\text{force}}]\). Dense, non-symmetric: body–body RSM point-Stokeslets + pili \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) segment blocks + cross terms. Factored once by LU (decomposition) and reused.

\([3N_{\text{tot}}\times 3N_{\text{force}}]\)。稠密非对称：胞体 RSM 点 Stokeslet + 纤毛 \(\mathbf{M}_1,\mathbf{M}_2\) 段块 + 交叉项。用 LU（decomposition）一次分解并复用。

\(\mathbf{B}\) — forces → balance— 力 → 平衡

\([n_{\text{state}}\times n_{\text{force}}]\). Rows 1–3 sum forces; rows 4–6 are \(\sum w_i\,\mathbf{r}_i\times\mathbf{f}_i\) (trapezoidal \(w_i\)); per-pilus rows are the Kirchhoff moment balance, scaled by \(\ell_k^{3}\).

\([n_{\text{state}}\times n_{\text{force}}]\)。第 1–3 行求和力；第 4–6 行为 \(\sum w_i\,\mathbf{r}_i\times\mathbf{f}_i\)（梯形权 \(w_i\)）；逐纤毛行为 Kirchhoff 力矩平衡，乘 \(\ell_k^{3}\)。

The assembly forms \(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\) by the mobility solve \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{w}}^{-1}\mathbf{Q}_{\text{trans,corr}}\) (rotlet-corrected) stacked with \(\widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{twist}}\mathbf{Q}_{\text{twist}}\); then \(\mathbf{L}=-\mathbf{B}\,\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\). Finally Step 7 overwrites each root's 2–3 rows with the clamped / free-torsion kinematic constraint via the Euler Jacobian \(\mathbf{J}_{\text{euler}}\).

组装时由迁移率求解 \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{w}}^{-1}\mathbf{Q}_{\text{trans,corr}}\)（经 rotlet 修正）与 \(\widetilde{\mathbf{A}}_{\text{twist}}\mathbf{Q}_{\text{twist}}\) 堆叠得 \(\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\)，再得 \(\mathbf{L}=-\mathbf{B}\,\mathbf{A}\mathbf{Q}\)。最后第 7 步用 Euler Jacobian \(\mathbf{J}_{\text{euler}}\) 将各根部 2–3 行替换为 clamped / free-torsion 运动学约束。

From Curvature to Elastic Forces从曲率到弹性力

1 · Constitutive law. Given the curvature deviations \(\kappa_i\) from the previous slide, the elastic restoring moment per unit length is simply

1 · 本构关系。由上一页的曲率偏差 \(\kappa_i\)，单位长度弹性恢复力矩为

\[ \mathbf{m}_{\text{el}} = EI\,(\kappa_1\mathbf{d}_1 + \kappa_2\mathbf{d}_2 + \kappa_3\mathbf{d}_3) \]

A single \(EI\) suffices because the torsion factor \(1/(1+\sigma)=GJ/EI\) is already folded into \(\kappa_3\).

用单一 \(EI\) 即可——扭转因子 \(1/(1+\sigma)=GJ/EI\) 已并入 \(\kappa_3\)。

2 · Discretization. The \(\kappa_i\) are evaluated on node-centred directors (segment-mean + SVD polar projection) with finite differences (\(\Delta s=1/N_k\)) — reusing the same nodal frames as the mobility assembly, so geometry stays consistent.

2 · 离散化。\(\kappa_i\) 在节点处 director（段均值 + SVD 极分解投影）上以有限差分（\(\Delta s=1/N_k\)）求值——复用与迁移率组装相同的节点标架，保持几何一致。

3 · Force / torque balance. On the inextensible rod, internal force \(\mathbf{F}\) and couple \(\mathbf{T}\) satisfy (Cortez 2018, Kirchhoff form):

3 · 力 / 力矩平衡。不可伸长杆上，内力 \(\mathbf{F}\) 与内力偶 \(\mathbf{T}\) 满足（Cortez 2018，Kirchhoff 形式）：

\[ \mathbf{f}+\frac{\partial\mathbf{F}}{\partial s}=\mathbf{0},\qquad \boldsymbol{\tau}+\frac{\partial\mathbf{T}}{\partial s}+\frac{\partial\mathbf{X}}{\partial s}\times\mathbf{F}=\mathbf{0} \]

so the fluid force/torque densities \(\mathbf{f},\boldsymbol{\tau}\) the rod feeds back are arclength derivatives of \(\mathbf{F},\mathbf{T}\) — assembled into the moment-balance rows of \(\mathbf{B}\) and the RHS \(\mathbf{R}\).

因此杆反馈给流体的力/力矩密度 \(\mathbf{f},\boldsymbol{\tau}\) 是 \(\mathbf{F},\mathbf{T}\) 的弧长导数 —— 装配进 \(\mathbf{B}\) 的力矩平衡行与右端项 \(\mathbf{R}\)。

Root handling: \(\kappa_1(1),\kappa_2(1)\) are zeroed (bending locked). For free_torsion, \(\kappa_3(1)\) is kept so the torsional elastic moment drives \(\dot\psi_1\); clamped zeros it and the BC row takes over.

根部处理：\(\kappa_1(1),\kappa_2(1)\) 置零（弯曲锁定）。free_torsion 下保留 \(\kappa_3(1)\)，由扭转弹性力矩驱动 \(\dot\psi_1\)；clamped 则置零并由边界条件行接管。

Brownian Dynamics — Effective Mobility布朗动力学 — 有效迁移率

Thermal motion is added by operator splitting around the deterministic solve:

热运动通过在确定性求解外层算符分裂引入：

  1. Integrate the deterministic coupled system with ode15s.
  2. At each Brownian node, compute the full-system effective body mobility.
  3. Apply a correlated 6-D translation–rotation kick (next slide).
  1. 用 ode15s 积分确定性耦合系统。
  2. 在每个布朗节点计算全系统有效胞体迁移率。
  3. 施加关联的 6 维平动–转动扰动（见下一页）。

Why full-system? The body never diffuses in isolation — every pilus segment adds drag and hydrodynamic coupling, so the mobility must come from the assembled \(\mathbf{L}\), not an isolated body resistance.

为何用全系统？胞体从不孤立扩散——每段纤毛都增加阻力与水动力耦合，故迁移率须取自组装后的 \(\mathbf{L}\)，而非孤立胞体阻力。

Probe the same cached LU with six unit generalized body loads \(-\mathbf{e}_i\); the top \(6\times6\) of the responses is the effective body mobility:

用 6 个单位广义胞体载荷 \(-\mathbf{e}_i\) 探测同一缓存 LU，取响应前 \(6\times6\) 即有效胞体迁移率：

\[ \mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}(:,i) = \big(\mathbf{L}^{-1}(-\mathbf{e}_i)\big)_{1:6},\ \ i=1,\dots,6 \]

\(\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}\) is symmetrized as \(\tfrac12(\mathbf{M}+\mathbf{M}^{\mathsf T})\) and its positive-definiteness asserted at runtime — it then feeds the fluctuation–dissipation step on the next slide.

\(\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}\) 对称化为 \(\tfrac12(\mathbf{M}+\mathbf{M}^{\mathsf T})\) 并在运行时校验正定性——随后进入下一页的涨落–耗散步骤。

Fluctuation–Dissipation: the Brownian Kick涨落–耗散：布朗扰动

Restoring dimensions on \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}\), the fluctuation–dissipation theorem fixes the effective diffusion tensor:

对 \(\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}^{\ast}\) 恢复量纲后，由涨落–耗散定理确定有效扩散张量：

\[ \mathbf{D}_{\text{eff}} = k_B T\,\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff,dim}} \]

Its Cholesky factor \(\mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\) "colours" white noise so the random increment carries the correct correlations:

其 Cholesky 因子 \(\mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\) 为白噪声“着色”，使随机增量带有正确的关联：

\[ \mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}^{\mathsf T} = 2\,\mathbf{D}_{\text{eff}} \]

One Brownian step is the correlated 6-D translation–rotation kick:

单个布朗步即关联的 6 维平动–转动扰动：

\[ \delta\mathbf{U} = \mathbf{L}_{\text{eff}}\,\boldsymbol{\eta}\,\sqrt{\Delta t_B},\quad \boldsymbol{\eta}\sim\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{0},\mathbf{I}_6) \]

Split into \(\delta\mathbf{U}=[\delta\mathbf{X},\delta\boldsymbol{\Theta}]\), it is applied between deterministic ode15s sub-intervals; the rotation part \(\delta\boldsymbol{\Theta}\) updates the body quaternion.

分为 \(\delta\mathbf{U}=[\delta\mathbf{X},\delta\boldsymbol{\Theta}]\)，施加于确定性 ode15s 子区间之间；转动部分 \(\delta\boldsymbol{\Theta}\) 更新胞体四元数。

The configuration-dependent Itô drift \(k_B T\,\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}\) is computed explicitly by six-coordinate finite differences of the SPD-projected body mobility. A logged RNG seed makes every stochastic run reproducible.

构型依赖的 Itô 漂移 \(k_B T\,\nabla\!\cdot\!\mathbf{M}_{\text{eff}}\) 通过对 SPD 投影后的胞体迁移率做六坐标有限差分显式计算。记录的随机种子使每次随机运行可复现。

Summary总结

Key Takeaways要点回顾

What it delivers能力

  • A fully coupled body–pili elastohydrodynamic solver in Stokes flow.
  • RSM + RSS + rotlet hydrodynamics with Kirchhoff elasticity and quaternion body motion.
  • Optional Brownian dynamics, background flow, and motor-driven flagella.
  • Stokes 流中完全耦合的胞体–纤毛弹性流体动力学求解器。
  • RSM + RSS + rotlet 流体力学，结合 Kirchhoff 弹性与四元数胞体运动。
  • 可选布朗动力学、背景流与马达驱动鞭毛。

Why it is robust为何稳健

  • Consistent nondimensionalization & Walker-scaling homogeneity.
  • Configuration-driven, reproducible, batch-capable.
  • Publication-grade, multi-format outputs.
  • 一致的无量纲化与 Walker 尺度齐次性。
  • 配置驱动、可复现、支持批处理。
  • 出版级多格式输出。
