# v9.0.0: Optional thermal fluctuations of the pili (brownian.pili_thermal /
#   cfg.enable_pili_brown, default off). The first and second bending modes of
#   every pilus receive thermal forces sampled jointly with the body from the
#   coupled mobility and applied as a force density on the pilus centrelines;
#   these modes reach equipartition. Twist and higher bending modes stay
#   deterministic. For passive, naturally straight pili with clamped roots
#   (free_torsion is rejected). Needs a Brownian interval of at most a
#   quarter of the fastest thermal relaxation time (0.002 s for 3 um
#   filaments). Results with the option off are unchanged bit for bit.
#   No new dependency.
#

#   - Interactive HTML figure export (exportgraphics → .html) requires MATLAB R2026a or later; PNG/PDF work from R2020a (exportgraphics).

#     batch_sweep/batch_repeat_brownian_HPC_Linux.m instead of the originals.
